# Student

*You are a student in a department.* LRMS is mainly for the people who look after and use laboratory resources. As a student, you have an account mainly so that others can book and act **on your behalf**, and so the register can record who used what.

Read [Getting started](00-getting-started.md) for signing in and your profile.

## What you see

After signing in you land on **Approvals**. Your menu is short: **Approvals**, **Categories**, and **Profile & password**.

![Where a student lands](img/student/01-landing.jpg)

- **Approvals** lists anything routed to you or raised by you. For most students it stays empty.
- **Categories** explains the kinds of resources the university records and the details each one keeps.
- **Profile & password**: your details, theme and text size, and your password.

## Booking a lab or a machine

Students don't book directly. **Ask your advisor or lecturer.** When they book, the form has an **On behalf of students** field where they note your name or ID. The lab's custodian sees who the booking is for.

## Screens outside your role

The register, the dashboard, schedules and lab states belong to staff roles. If you open one (from a link someone shared, say), LRMS tells you plainly instead of showing an error:

![Screens outside your role](img/student/03-not-your-role.jpg)

This is a boundary, not a fault. If you need more access for a project, ask your department head or the system administrator.
