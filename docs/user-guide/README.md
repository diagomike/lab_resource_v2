# ASTU Lab Resources: user guide

ASTU Lab Resources (LRMS) is the university's register of laboratory resources: every lab, store, machine and part, who owns it, who is responsible for it, and what condition it is in. The same system also handles the work around those resources:

- updating a lab's condition and getting it approved;
- planning what each lab *should* hold;
- buying what is missing and receiving it into the store;
- booking rooms and machines;
- hosting outside institutions.

Every change is recorded, with who made it and when.

## Find your role

| You are… | What you mainly do in LRMS | Your chapter |
|---|---|---|
| A **lab custodian** (ARA / lab responsible) | Keep your labs' records true, report what broke, plan what the lab should hold, accept deliveries, approve bookings of your rooms | [Lab custodian](02-custodian.md) |
| A **department head** | Approve your labs' updates and plans, compile purchase requests, manage your department's people | [Department head](03-department-head.md) |
| **Staff / lecturer** | Look up resources, book a lab or a machine, raise a need | [Staff / lecturer](04-staff.md) |
| A **student** | Your profile; bookings go through your advisor | [Student](05-student.md) |
| A **dean, the AVP or the College Managing Director** | Approve purchase requests; the AVP also handles outside institutions' requests | [Dean / AVP / CMD](06-dean-avp.md) |
| The **procurement officer** | Approve purchases and move orders through to delivery | [Procurement](07-procurement.md) |
| The **store keeper** | Receive deliveries into the Main Store and hand them over to labs | [Store keeper](08-store-keeper.md) |
| The **ICT maintenance officer** | See, read-only, which devices in every department need maintenance, and plan for it | [ICT maintenance](12-ict-maintenance.md) |
| The **system administrator** | Set up the org chart, people, categories and access views | [System administrator](09-system-admin.md) |
| The **property administrator** | Categories and access views, university-wide | [Property administrator](10-property-admin.md) |
| An **outside organisation** | Request a workshop or training, pay the quote, track it | [Public portal](11-external-portal.md) |

Many people hold more than one role. Most custodians are also staff, and most heads are also staff. Read every chapter that applies to you.

## How to read the steps

- Steps are numbered in the order you do them.
- In the screenshots, **red outlines** show what to click or look at. Where there are several, the **numbered red circles** match the numbered steps or notes below the picture.
- *Good to know* boxes explain what the system does behind the scenes, such as why an edit waited for approval or why something was refused.
- Screen names in **bold** match the menu on the left of the app.

The screenshots come from a test copy of the real data: the Computer Science and Engineering and Chemical Engineering labs. Personal email addresses are masked (`a•••••@gmail.com`).
