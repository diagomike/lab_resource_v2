# ASTU Lab Resources: user guide

ASTU Lab Resources (LRMS) is the university's record of its laboratories: every lab, store, machine and part, who looks after it, and what condition it is in. Around that record it does five jobs:

- **know what we have**: every lab and store across ASTU, and what is in them;
- **share it**: lend or move things between labs and the Main Store;
- **buy what's missing**: labs ask, the head builds a purchase request, it is approved and ordered, and what arrives goes into the store and then the labs;
- **book it**: rooms, machines and weekly classes;
- **outside access**: institutions outside ASTU ask for rooms or a sample analysis, get a quote and pay.

Every change is recorded, with who made it and when.

## Find your role

| You are… | What you mainly do in LRMS | Your chapter |
|---|---|---|
| A **lab custodian** (ARA / lab responsible) | Keep your labs' records true, send their changes to your head, ask for what they need, decide bookings of your rooms | [Lab custodian](02-custodian.md) |
| A **department head** | Approve your labs' changes, add labs and choose who runs them, build purchase requests, look after your categories and people | [Department head](03-department-head.md) |
| A college's **ADAA** (Associate Dean of Academic Affairs) | Add the college's stores and choose their store keepers; see the whole college | [ADAA](04-adaa.md) |
| A **dean, the AVP or the College Managing Director** | Approve purchase requests and permanent transfers; the AVP also handles outside institutions' requests | [Dean / AVP / CMD](06-dean-avp.md) |
| The **procurement officer** | Give purchases their final approval and follow orders until they arrive | [Procurement](07-procurement.md) |
| The **store keeper** | Load what arrived into the Main Store, and move it to the labs | [Store keeper](08-store-keeper.md) |
| The **property administrator** | Record what arrived, approve movements in and out of the Main Store, look after the university-wide categories | [Property administrator](10-property-admin.md) |
| The **system administrator** | Set up the organisation, people and roles; correct records | [System administrator](09-system-admin.md) |
| An **outside organisation** | Request rooms or an analysis, pay the quote, follow it | [Public portal](11-external-portal.md) |

Some people hold more than one role, a head who also runs a lab, for example. Read every chapter that applies to you.

## How to read the steps

- Steps are numbered in the order you do them.
- In the screenshots, **red outlines** show what to click or look at.
- *Good to know* boxes explain what the system does behind the scenes, such as why an edit waited for approval or why something was refused.
- Screen names in **bold** match the menu on the left of the app.

The screenshots come from a test copy of the real data: the Computer Science and Engineering and Chemical Engineering labs.
