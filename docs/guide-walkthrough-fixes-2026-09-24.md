## Added at your request: the ICT Maintenance Office

A **view-only, university-wide** post, so ICT can see which devices in each department need maintenance and plan for it. It needed no new role or code, only configuration the app already supports:

- **Org node:** ASTU → **ICT Maintenance Office** (Office, code `ICT`).
- **Person:** **ICT Maintenance Officer**, `ict.maintenance@astu.edu.et` / `astu1234` (a placeholder role account like the others), role **Staff**, home unit ICT Maintenance Office.
- **Access view:** **"ICT maintenance — every department"**, University-wide and **read only**, assigned to that person. Views widen reads only, and Staff never writes resources, so the officer can look but not touch.
- **What they use:**
  - the Dashboard (Needs attention, Where the problems are, Breakdown by owning unit or lab);
  - the Register, filtered by status (Broken, Maintenance, Impaired);
  - Change log for history.

On the clone it was created through the admin UI (it's in the guide's admin chapter). In the **dev DB** `lrms_v2` it was added by the new idempotent `prisma/seed-ict-maintenance.ts`, which `prisma/seed.ts` now also calls, so a reseed keeps it. Not yet possible: pinning a *saved filter* such as "only broken devices" to a view. The Access views editor doesn't author `extraFilters` yet, a deferral recorded in `AccessViewsPage.tsx`.

## Added at your request: approval emails (25 September 2026)

The internal approval flows had no notifications. Now everyone hears when something waits for them, and requesters hear the outcome.

- **`lib/server/mail/notify.ts`** (new): `notify(userIds, actorId, notice)` resolves user ids to **active** accounts, never emails the person who just acted, and sends one at a time. Like every mail here, it's only called **after** the write commits, and a failure is logged, never raised. The layout matches the external-request mails, with a button into the right screen. Everything typed by people is escaped.
- **Purchasing** (`purchasing.ts`):
  - the next approver is told on compile, resubmit and each approval (dean, AVP, CMD, Procurement);
  - the raiser is told when it's approved, rejected, sent back, cancelled by procurement, at each pipeline stage, and when it's fully received;
  - store keepers are told when it arrives in store;
  - the waiting approver is told when the raiser withdraws it;
  - a need's raiser is told when it's declined.
- **Lab commits** (`lab-versions.ts`): the head is told on submit (draft or ideal). The custodian is told when it's approved, sent back or refused as stale.
- **Transfers and handovers** (`approvals.ts`): the next step's holder is told (including "confirm receipt"). The requester is told when it's done, rejected or stale, and the waiting approver when it's withdrawn.
- **Bookings** (`reservations.ts`): the lab's custodian is told about a staff request and a withdrawal. The requester is told when it's approved, declined or cancelled. Class and external holds are excluded (external requests have their own mail).
- **Tests:** `MAIL_DISABLED=true` (set by `vitest.config.ts`) makes `send` a no-op, so no spec reaches SMTP. Four specs capture the mail by mocking `../mail/mail` and assert exact recipients and subjects:
  - purchasing: each of the five hand-offs, then approved; sent back;
  - lab-versions: submit goes to the head, approval to the custodian;
  - scheduling: a request goes to the custodian, the decline to the requester, and a custodian's own booking mails nobody;
  - approvals: only the first approver is told; the next one only when it reaches them; the rejection goes to the requester.
  - 528/528, and `next build` is clean.
- **Live on :3100** (mail sink): PR-2026-003 mailed dean → AVP → CMD → Procurement in turn, then the head ("is approved", "Buyer found", "On delivery", "Arrived at the main store"), plus the store keeper ("has arrived at the main store"). One email per hand-off, none to the actor. The rendered email was checked.
- **Guide:** Getting started §8 "Emails you'll get" lists every email, and the approvers' chapter says they're emailed.

## Added at your request: a per-person email switch (25 September 2026)

Notification emails can be turned off for anyone, and they start **off for the 17 CSE ARAs**.

- **Schema:** `User.emailNotifications` (default `true`), from the additive migration `20260925090000_user_email_notifications`. It's applied to `lrms_v2` and the clone; `migrate diff` against `lrms_v2` shows no difference.
- **What it controls:** `notify()` sends only to people who have it on. That covers every approval and outcome email, and `mailStaff` (external-request staff mail) too. Invitations, password resets and mail to outside requesters are unaffected, since they're how someone gets in or has no account.
- **Self:** Profile → **Email notifications**, via `POST /api/auth/notifications`. The checkbox changes at once, and the session is refreshed. `SessionUserDto.emailNotifications`.
- **For someone else:** People & roles → Manage → **Email notifications**, via `POST /api/people/[id]/notifications` → `people.setEmailNotifications`. It has the same reach as every other staff action (`assertMayManageStaff`): an admin for anyone, and a head for their department's custodians and staff. Anyone may set their own. `PersonDto.emailNotifications`.
- **ARAs off:** `prisma/cse-lab-data.ts` creates them with `emailNotifications: false`, and all 17 were switched off in `lrms_v2` and the clone (17 off, 14 on).
- **Tests:** in `people.spec.ts`:
  - on by default; a head can switch their own custodian; an unrelated head gets 403; an admin can switch anyone; anyone can switch their own;
  - `notify` skips an opted-out recipient.
  - 530/530, and `next build` is clean.
- **Live on :3100:** Ali's Profile shows it off; on → reload → on; off → reload → off. The admin's Manage dialog shows Ali's switch off. Both checkboxes update at once and are corrected by the reload (the first try found a click that seemed to do nothing until the refresh landed).
- **Guide:** Getting started §7 and §8, the admin chapter's Manage, and the head chapter.
