# Act 5 · The dean sends it back, then approves

**Sign in as** `coeec.dean@astu.edu.et`. Any approver can approve, reject, or send a request back to the head with a note.

## 1. Send it back

Open **Approvals → Purchasing**. The card shows the chain with the dean's step highlighted, the lines, and the history. Press **Send back for revision** (3).

![A purchase request at the dean's step](img/05-dean/01-waiting.jpg)

Write what's missing (unit costs) and confirm.

![Send back for revision, with a note](img/05-dean/02-send-back.jpg)

## 2. The head revises (sign in as the CSE head)

On **Purchasing**, the request is *Sent back for revision*, with the dean's note on top. Press **Edit & resubmit** (1).

![The head sees why it came back](img/05-dean/03-head-sent-back.jpg)

Fill in **Est. unit cost** on each line, and press **Resubmit** (1). The chain starts again at the dean.

![Add the costs and resubmit](img/05-dean/04-head-revise.jpg)

## 3. The dean approves (sign in as the dean again)

Back on **Approvals → Purchasing**, press **Approve**, add a note, and confirm.

> **Good to know**
> - **Emails:** the head is emailed "PR-2026-001 was sent back for revision" (with the note); after resubmitting, the dean is emailed again; after the dean approves, the **AVP** is.
> - The history keeps every send-back and its note, even though the chain itself restarts.
