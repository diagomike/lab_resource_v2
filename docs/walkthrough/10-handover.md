# Act 10 · The head approves, the custodian accepts

A handover needs two yeses: the receiving department's head, then the custodian who will be answerable for it.

## 1. The head approves (sign in as the CSE head)

Open **Approvals → Transfers**. Each handover shows what, from where, to where, and its steps. Press **Approve** (1) on both.

![Head: a handover into your department](img/10-handover/01-head-approve.jpg)

## 2. The custodian accepts (sign in as Ali)

Open **Approvals → Transfers**. Press **Accept into my custody** (1) on both.

![Custodian: accept it into your custody](img/10-handover/02-custodian-accept.jpg)

## 3. In the lab

In **Register**, B510-R8 now holds 25 workstations and the projector, in Ali's custody and owned by CSE:

![The new workstations and the projector, now in B510-R8](img/10-handover/03-in-the-lab.jpg)

On **Lab states → Ideal**, every gap is 0 and nothing is left under *Missing*. The lab has what it should hold. *Needs attention* still counts what's broken: the replacement parts are in the store for when Ali swaps them in.

![Ideal vs Current: the workstation gap is closed](img/10-handover/04-gap-closed.jpg)

> **Good to know**
> - **Emails:** the head's approval emails Ali (off in this story); the acceptance emails the store keeper, "Your transfer is done".
> - Custody and ownership move only when the custodian accepts: until then the store keeper still holds the items.
