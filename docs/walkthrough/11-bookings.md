# Act 11 · The lecturer's booking

## 1. The custodian decides (sign in as Ali)

The lecturer's request from Act 3 is under **Approvals → Lab bookings** (and **Schedule → My labs**). Add a note if you like, then press **Approve** (1). **Decline** needs no more than a note to say why.

![Custodian: a booking request for your lab](img/11-bookings/01-request.jpg)

![Confirmed on the lab's calendar](img/11-bookings/02-calendar.jpg)

![Staff: your booking is confirmed](img/11-bookings/03-my-bookings.jpg)
